interviewer3d.glb is the "mpfb.glb" example avatar from TalkingHead
(https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead), created with Blender + MPFB,
licensed CC0 (public domain). Compressed for the web (WebP textures,
meshopt geometry) with glTF-Transform. The T-shirt is recoloured at runtime
by avatar3d_player.js.
